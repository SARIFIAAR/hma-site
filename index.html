The Advisory

The Approach

The depth behind the work.

13+years of dedicated study & practice
15,000+hours of research & application
2,000+pages of written research

HMA brings together the science of human behaviour with the realities of leadership, performance, relationships, decision-making and everyday life — a practical approach to understanding and intentionally architecting what matters most to you.

Its intellectual foundation draws from behavioural and applied psychology, neurobiology and neuroscience, human performance, leadership development, decision science, behavioural change, habit formation and executive advisory — alongside established approaches studied over the years, including Hintsa Performance’s human-performance methodology and relevant teachings from Harvard Medical School Executive Education and Coaching.

These disciplines are not applied as a fixed formula. They inform the questions we ask, the perspectives we explore and the way each Architect’s individual circumstances are understood.
